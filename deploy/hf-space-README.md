# BreatheEasy: Urban Air Quality Assistant

Turns the hourly air-quality forecast into a plan for each person in your household (school run, walks, masks, when to open windows), with a Gemini briefing in English or Hindi.

Source code, approach and assumptions: https://github.com/vamshimorlawar/breatheeasy-aqi-assistant
